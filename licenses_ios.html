Open-Source Licences (iPhone)

The Glimpse iPhone app is built on open-source software. This page lists every library that ships with the app, with its licence and, where the licence asks for it, the copyright notice and the full licence text. Thank you to everyone who wrote it.

The libraries of the Android app are on their own page.
